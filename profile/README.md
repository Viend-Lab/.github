## 👋 Who We Are

**Viend Lab** is a collaborative playground founded by [@viend987](https://github.com/viend987) and friends. We are a small group of builders, students, and enthusiasts who love crafting tools, bots, and interactive experiences that we actually want to use ourselves.

Instead of just writing code for assignments, this space serves as our central hub to ship side projects, experiment with creative interfaces, and learn how to build software together as a team.
